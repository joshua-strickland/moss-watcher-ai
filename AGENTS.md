# Moss Watcher Workspace Rules

You are collaborating on **Moss Watcher**, an artist and creative project combining **Melodic House music production** (120–124 BPM) with **"Enchanted Woods" short-form visual content**.

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Core Vibe:** *"The feeling of finding a secret garden in a magical forest."* Content should evoke curiosity, exploration, melancholic hope, wonder, and awe.
- **The Explorer Archetype:** Listeners and viewers step into the shoes of a silent explorer—an everyday person in modern clothes stumbling upon sacred glowing stones, ancient rivers, or surreal nature for the first time.
- **Brand Keywords:** Moss, bioluminescence, ancient, peaceful, alien-nature.
- **Visual Palette:** Deep forest greens, teal, and soft amber; macro moss textures, floating pollen, glowing blue fog, and cinematic surrealism.
- **Auditory Signature:** Melodic House rooted in minor keys (e.g., Eb Minor, F Minor, G Minor) blended with organic nature Foley (wind, rain, flowing water, frogs, solitary bird chirps, footsteps on dry leaves).
- **Avoid:** High-stress visuals, aggressive EDM tropes (harsh stadium snares, abrasive festival drops), and generic glossy "AI-look" imagery.

## Project Strategy & Priorities
- **70 / 30 Effort Split:** Balance effort at roughly **70% music production** and **30% visual/social content**. Prioritize work that moves tracks toward "Finished" status.
- **Burnout Prevention:** Structure plans around **3-week sprints** and **batch** video/prompt generation to minimize context-switching overhead. If music hasn't been worked on in 3+ days, deprioritize video editing in favor of track progress.

## Workspace Map & Skills
Specialized procedures live as auto-discovered skills in `.agents/skills/`:
- **Song Analysis (`.agents/skills/song-analysis/SKILL.md`):** Authoring, validating, and mining Melodic House `.yaml` track breakdowns in `song-analysis/` and running STFT audio analysis via `python/frequency-analysis.py`.
- **Melodic House Production (`.agents/skills/melodic-house-production/SKILL.md`):** Composing, arranging, sound-designing, and mixing tracks using the reference playbooks in `arrangement-and-sound-design/`.
- **Visual & Social Content (`.agents/skills/visual-and-social/SKILL.md`):** Designing "Explorer" lore, visual generation prompts (`visual/prompts/`), beat-synced storyboards (`visual/storyboards/`), and high-retention social hooks.
