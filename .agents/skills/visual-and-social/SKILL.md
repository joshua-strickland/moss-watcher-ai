# Visual Worldbuilding & Social Media Workflow

Use this skill to design **Enchanted Woods** visual assets and short-form video hooks synchronized with Moss Watcher's Melodic House music.

## 1. Visual Worldbuilding & Lore ("The Explorer")
- **Protagonist:** A silent explorer—an ordinary person in modern clothes stumbling upon a hidden, ancient, bioluminescent corner of nature.
- **Core Settings:**
  - *Enchanted Forest:* Dense moss carpets, towering ancient trees, wild surreal bioluminescent plants, and blue glowing fog.
  - *Moonlit Lake / Ancient River:* Calm reflective water under a bright blue moon, mossy riverbanks, mist drifting low over the surface.
  - *Twilight Field:* Tall grass with surreal, trippy fireflies and peaceful, non-threatening woodland animals with softly glowing eyes.
- **Recurring Magical Artifacts:**
  - *Pulse Stones:* Ancient weathered rocks engraved with runes/symbols that glow in sync with musical transitions.
  - *River Spirits:* Small floating motes of light flitting gently above water or through forest clearings.
- **Visual Style & Color Palette:**
  - **Palette:** Deep forest greens, teal, and soft warm amber accents.
  - **Camera & Texture:** Tactile macro close-ups (dewy moss, spores, floating pollen, macro-bioluminescence) paired with calm, wide cinematic compositions.
  - **Guardrails:** Aim for grounded **cinematic surrealism**—natural lighting physics, filmic grain, and peaceful pacing. Never use frenetic edits, high-stress imagery, or plastic/oversaturated generic "AI art" aesthetics.

## 2. Audio-Visual Sync & High-Retention Social Mechanics
Short-form videos (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) should fuse audio transitions directly with visual payoffs:
1. **The "Wait for the Drop" Glow-Up:** Time the track's pre-drop vacuum (where drums/synths cut out for 1–2 beats and a dry Foley sound hits) to an anticipation cue, then sync the **Bar 1, Beat 1** bass/drop entry with a visual illumination (e.g., a *Pulse Stone* igniting, bioluminescent flora lighting up across the forest floor, or fog parting).
2. **Tactile ASMR Intros:** Layer crisp foreground nature sounds (footsteps crunching on dry leaves, fingers brushing wet moss, water droplets) over the low-passed Melodic House intro to hook viewers in the first 2 seconds.
3. **Seamless Loops:** Match the closing camera motion/frame and ambient audio tail to the opening frame so short-form clips loop invisibly and boost watch-time retention.
4. **Explorer Roleplay Captions:** Write concise, curiosity-driven captions and on-screen text from a first- or second-person Explorer POV (inviting viewers to pause, wander, or share what they'd do upon finding the place), paired with Melodic House and ambient-nature tags.

## 3. Workspace File Conventions
- **Prompts (`visual/prompts/`):** Store reusable image- and video-generation prompts as Markdown files named by scene or track slug (specifying subject, setting, lighting/palette, camera lens/motion, and negative constraints).
- **Storyboards (`visual/storyboards/`):** Store beat-synced shot lists mapping timestamps or `Bar.Beat.16th` coordinates to visual action, audio cues, and on-screen hook text.
- **Batching Reminder:** Group prompt writing and storyboard planning into focused batches so visual work stays within the project's **30% video effort budget**.
