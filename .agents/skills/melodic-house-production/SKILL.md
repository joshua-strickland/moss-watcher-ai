# Melodic House Production & Sound Design

Use this workflow and its linked reference guides when planning tracks, designing patches/chains, or troubleshooting mixes for the **Moss Watcher** sound.

## Style Profile
- **Reference Artists:** Lane 8, Ben Böhmer, Sultan + Shepard, EMBRZ, Ocula.
- **Tempo:** **120–124 BPM** — driving 4/4 pulse with enough space between kicks for long reverb tails and organic Foley to breathe.
- **Keys:** Prefer minor keys (**F Minor**, **G Minor**, **Eb Minor**, **A Minor**, **C Minor**) for a *"melancholic but hopeful"* tone. F Minor (~43 Hz root) and G Minor (~49 Hz root) sit squarely in the optimal 40–60 Hz club sub-bass pocket; deploy major keys (Eb Major, C Major) selectively for pure "glow"/awe tracks.
- **Moss Watcher Signature:** Blend analog-warm synthesis (u-he Diva, Serum/Vital, Omnisphere) with continuous environmental Foley (wind, rain, rivers) sidechained to the kick ("atmospheric breath"), plus dry intermittent fauna/forest hits (bird chirps, frogs, footsteps on dry leaves, twig snaps) placed in transition vacuums.

## Composition & Review Checklist
When outlining or reviewing a Moss Watcher track arrangement:
1. Specify **BPM**, **Key** (noting fundamental sub frequency), and **16/32-bar section timeline**.
2. Plan **low-pass/high-pass filter automation arcs** across Intro → Build → Drop rather than static loops.
3. Explicitly script **pre-drop drop-outs (`Bar.Beat.16th`)** and choose a **dry signature nature Foley hit** for the pre-drop vacuum so it pairs cleanly with a visual "glow-up" moment for short-form video.
